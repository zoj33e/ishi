## self_atari
The learner's own move left its group with one liberty (`last_liberty`), so
the opponent can capture it there. groups[0] is that group, including
played_move.
Good example (en): "After C1, your stone had only one liberty left, at B1.
The opponent can capture it by playing there." Question: "Where would have
been a better move?"
Good example (th): "พอลงที่ C1 หมากของเราเหลือลมหายใจช่องเดียวที่ B1
ฝ่ายตรงข้ามลงตรงนั้นก็กินได้เลย"
