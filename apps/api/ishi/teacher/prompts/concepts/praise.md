## praise
lesson_type "praise": the opponent's threat_move made a threat (praise_kind
`answered_atari`: put groups[0] in atari; `answered_cut`: threatened a cut)
and the learner answered correctly with played_move. Say what they did right
in one sentence and why it worked in one sentence. No question, no hints.
Good example (en): "The opponent played D4 and put your stone in atari. You
answered with E5, and your stones got out of danger."
Good example (th): "ฝ่ายตรงข้ามลง D4 ทำให้หมากของเราติดอาตาริ เราตอบด้วย E5
หมากของเราเลยพ้นอันตราย"
