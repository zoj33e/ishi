## cut_disconnect
Two learner groups (groups g1, g2) were joined by one point (`cut_point`,
also `connect_move`: the answer, do not name it). After played_move the
opponent can cut them apart and both get weaker.
Good example (en): "Your two groups marked with triangles were joined by only
one point. After B2, the opponent can cut them apart." Question: "Where
should you have connected?"
Good example (th): "หมากสองกลุ่มของเราต่อกันได้แค่จุดเดียว พอเราไปลง B2
ฝ่ายตรงข้ามก็ตัดแยกได้"
