## ignored_atari
The learner's group was in atari (one liberty) and was worth saving, but the
learner played elsewhere. groups[0] is the learner's group. `liberty_point`
is its last liberty, which is not always the saving move. Never say how the
group should be saved (extend, capture, connect, "play on the last liberty"):
finding that is the learner's task.
Good example (en): "Your 2 stones marked with triangles had only one liberty
left. This is atari: the opponent can capture them next move. You played G3,
so they were left in danger." Question: "How could you have saved them?"
Good example (th): "หมาก 2 เม็ดของเราที่มีสามเหลี่ยม เหลือลมหายใจช่องเดียว
แบบนี้เรียกว่าอาตาริ ฝ่ายตรงข้ามกินได้ในตาถัดไป แต่เราไปลงที่ G3"
